Liam — Book One

He's protected presidents, princes, and billionaires. But the one woman he's loved for seven years is the one assignment he never wanted to need.

Cassie Thorne has spent two years alone at the head of her late grandfather's empire — and three months looking over her shoulder. When her ex breaks into her home and leaves proof he's been watching her for weeks, there's only one call to make: her best friend Lily, whose big brother happens to run the most formidable private security firm in Texas.

Liam Blackstone fell for Cassie the day his little sister brought her home — and spent seven years keeping a careful, honorable distance. But when her voice breaks on a midnight phone call, seven years of discipline burn down in a single sentence: I'm bringing you home, Cassie.

A steamy, heart-pounding romantic suspense featuring a protective ex-SEAL hero, a heroine who saves herself, a found family worth fighting for, and a guaranteed happily-ever-after. No cliffhangers. No cheating. All heart.

Levi · Book Two · Coming Soon

Levi — Book Two

Investigative journalist Quinn Sutherland made her name doing the impossible: mapping a criminal no one could prove existed. Her reporting cleared a murdered man's name and earned her an invitation to a Blackstone wedding — along with one dance with the quiet second brother she can't stop thinking about, and a loose thread she can't put down. Because the ghost who built the Vale conspiracy had a partner. And her byline just told him she can see his shape.

Levi Blackstone is the steady one. The anchor. The man who takes the 3 a.m. calls so his family never has to. When Quinn's source vanishes, her career implodes in a single morning, and she discovers her own home has been silently owned for months, Levi drives ninety miles in seventy-eight minutes — and negotiates the most stubborn woman in Texas onto the ranch not as a protectee, but as a partner.

Their enemy doesn't send threats. He doesn't make demands. He buys the pipes — records, licenses, headlines — and rewrites the past itself, building a world where the Blackstones were never heroes at all. To beat a man who owns the truth, Quinn will have to do the one thing she was born for and the one thing that terrifies her: trust her instincts, her found family, and the con-man father she's spent twenty years refusing to read.

A slow-burn, steamy romantic suspense featuring a guarded protector hero, a razor-sharp heroine, the best worst father in Texas, and a guaranteed happily-ever-after. No cliffhangers. All heart.
